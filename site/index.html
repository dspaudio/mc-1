01 / Sound palette

From the first note
to the last kick.

Three melodic parts combine the rebuilt LA-PCM engine with a 303-inspired Acid engine. A fourth part brings 16 lanes of synthesized drums.

112 rebuilt LA-PCM sounds.
17 instrument families.

Version 1.2.0 replaces the entire LA bank: piano, nylon guitar, organ, mallets, harp, strings, woodwinds and brass. The 16 Acid sounds and eight synthesized drum kits are retained.

Real PCM attacks retain each instrument's character. Two phase-coherent harmonic frames synthesize the sustain without detuned beating or a repeated sample loop. This is LA-PCM, not a GM SoundFont player. All 24 source zones fit within the previous PCM budget.

Recordings come from Meta Open Sound and VSCO 2 Community Edition under CC0. Firmware and original patches remain GPL-3.0-only.

02 / Shape the space

Four FX banks.
More room to play.

Each part has independent sends to R/D1, C/F1, R/D2 and C/F2: two reverb/delay banks and two chorus/flanger banks, each with eight types. ENV switches between sends and global types. Optional Analog variance adds subtle variation to melodic sounds. Version 1.2.0 improves wet level and ROOM/HALL separation. The master stays volume and limiter only.

03 / Play it. Capture it.

Give the pattern
a performance.

Six arpeggiator modes

Up, Down, Up-Down, Order, Random and Chord. Set rate, octave range and gate, then latch the phrase. PLAY + REC writes the generated notes into the sequencer.

384 automation events

Turn a sound, level or FX knob during PLAY + REC to record its value at the current step. Refine an existing move or clear just the current pattern's automation.

04 / Stay in the groove

Small screen.
Direct decisions.

Orange shows the selection. Bright green follows playback. Slow turns stay precise; quick turns move through larger ranges faster.

PRESETS
Pick a melodic sound or drum kit. The large list closes after 500 ms of idle time.
ALGORITHM
Select one of 64 patterns on any part; queue the next pattern while playing.
SEQ → EDIT
Switch Pattern / Song mode. In Song, browse positions with knob 1; EDIT unlocks pattern, repeats and length; LFO toggles looping. Arrange up to 128 positions with 1–16 repeats each.
ENV
On a melodic sound, edit attack, decay, sustain and release. In FX, cycle the effects pages.
FX → ENV
Open the selected part’s four sends in 1.2.0: R/D1, C/F1, R/D2 and C/F2. ENV switches to the four global bank types.
ARP → LFO
Open the melodic arpeggiator and toggle latch. In GLOBAL MIXER, ARP mutes the selected part.
GLO
Cycle through Global Mixer, Groove, Demo, Knob Automation and MIDI. MIDI knobs set four track channels; LFO selects Internal, USB or TRS clock. BLE hardware support is unavailable.
PLAY + REC
Capture notes and parameter changes. Save the project to keep the performance.

Choose your firmware

Install 1.2.0 dual-core by default, or select the immutable original 1.0.2 single-core fallback. Verified local-file restoration of official V15 remains available.

1.2.0 saves format 8; 1.0.2 cannot load format 8. Switching firmware is neither project conversion nor a backup. Saving after a downgrade can overwrite prior banks.

Catchable CPU1 timeout or worker protocol failure disables the worker and recomputes on CPU0, without guaranteed full-load real-time audio. Fatal system faults stop audio and attempt UBOOT only when CPU1 is held safely. Power cycling retries the same image, not an automatic rollback; startup faults can persist. ROM WL80UBOOT is not the Web MIDI loader and cannot be recovered by this page: expert app-only recovery is required.

Original groovebox software. Not a Roland emulation; not affiliated with Roland or M-VAVE. No Roland ROMs, samples, patches, logos or compatibility formats are used. Every display image on this page is an actual shared-C render.

The replacement LA-PCM bank uses CC0 recordings: piano and nylon guitar from Meta Open Sound, recorded by Meta's Creative Audio team; 15 other instrument families from VSCO 2 Community Edition, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, with sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. See the Meta CC0 license, VSCO CC0 license, 24-zone source/processing manifest and full provenance. Voice presets process woodwinds, not human vocals. Firmware, original patches, drum synthesis and compositions remain GPL-3.0-only.
