01 / Sound palette

From the first note
to the last kick.

Three melodic parts combine LA-inspired synthesis, a 303-inspired engine and real instrument recordings. A fourth part brings 16 lanes of synthesized drums.

64 recorded-instrument presets.
16 real recordings.

Piano, organ, mallets, harp, strings, woodwinds and brass sit alongside 64 original synthesis patches. Fourteen sound families keep all 128 presets easy to browse.

The recorded sounds use CC0 samples from VSCO 2 Community Edition. Their default mix is sample-only; lower PCM mix to blend in the oscillator.

03 / Play it. Capture it.

Give the pattern
a performance.

Six arpeggiator modes

Up, Down, Up-Down, Order, Random and Chord. Set rate, octave range and gate, then latch the phrase. PLAY + REC writes the generated notes into the sequencer.

384 automation events

Turn a sound, level or FX knob during PLAY + REC to record its value at the current step. Refine an existing move or clear just the current pattern's automation.

04 / Stay in the groove

Small screen.
Direct decisions.

Orange shows the selection. Bright green follows playback. Slow turns stay precise; quick turns move through larger ranges faster.

PRESETS
Pick a melodic sound or drum kit. The large list closes after 500 ms of idle time.
ALGORITHM
Select a pattern on any part; queue the next pattern while playing.
FX → ENV
Open track effects, press FX again for master, then use ENV for amounts, types or filters.
ARP → LFO
Open the melodic arpeggiator and toggle latch. In GLOBAL MIXER, ARP mutes the selected part.
GLO
Cycle through Global Mixer, Groove, Demo and Knob Automation.
PLAY + REC
Capture notes and parameter changes. Save the project to keep the performance.

Original groovebox software. Not a Roland emulation; not affiliated with Roland or M-VAVE. No Roland ROMs, samples, patches, logos or compatibility formats are used. Every display image on this page is an actual shared-C render.

The 16 instrument recordings are from VSCO 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Distributed under CC0 1.0. See the source manifest. The recorded Voice presets process woodwinds; they are not human vocal recordings. Original synthesis, drum kits and demo composition remain distinct from the sample library.
