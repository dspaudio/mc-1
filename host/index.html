Drum page: SOUND / STEP / HIT / LEVEL. Melodic page: CUT / RES / DEC / PCM or GLIDE. FX opens the selected part’s R/D and C/F sends. ENV switches SENDS / TYPES; both type choices are global. ARP opens the arpeggiator; LFO toggles latch. Press GLO once for GLOBAL MIXER (PART1 / PART2 / PART3 / DRUMS), or three times for DEMO LIBRARY, then turn LOAD while stopped. GLO a fourth time opens automation; EDIT clears its current pattern. PLAY + REC records knob motion and generated arp notes. Turn slowly for precision or quickly for acceleration. PRESETS opens a list that closes after 500 ms. Turn with − / + or use the mouse wheel.
